Making your list

How do I add items?

Type in the field at the bottom and press Return; the keyboard stays up for the next item. As you type, the app suggests the rest of a name in grey: press Return to take it, or keep typing. Items you've had before, and common groceries, are listed underneath too: tap one to add it.

Why doesn't it add the same item twice?

Everything you've ever added is kept. Typing a name you've used before puts that item back on the list, with its aisles and notes, instead of making a copy.

What's "From last time"?

Things you've bought before that aren't on the list now. Tap one to put it back, or tap Add all to start a new list from all of them.

When does "Suggested" appear?

Once the app has seen you buy something at least twice, on different days, it learns how often you buy it. When most of that time has passed, the item appears under Suggested with the reason, such as "Usually every week". Purchases are recorded when you tap Finish after shopping, so suggestions start after a couple of shops.

How do I sort the list?

Tap the … button at the top right. Choose your own order, by aisle or by category, or tap Sort A to Z to put the list in alphabetical order. To drag items into your own order, tap Reorder, drag, then tap Done.

How do I delete an item?

The minus only takes an item off the list; it stays under "From last time" for next time. To delete it for good, press and hold it and tap Delete, swipe left on it under "From last time", or tap Delete in the item's editor. Undo appears for a few seconds in case of a slip.

Shopping

How do I start and finish a shop?

Tap the cart button beside the field to start. Tap an item to put it in the basket; tap it in the basket to put it back. Tap Finish when you're done: what you bought leaves the list. If some things weren't found, you can mark any you no longer need; the rest stay on the list for next time, marked "Left over" with the store and day. The screen stays on while you shop.

How do aisles work?

Each item can have an aisle for each store. While shopping, swipe left on an item (or press and hold it) to set or change its aisle at the chosen store, since stores move things round. Choose By aisle from the sort button to walk the list aisle by aisle.

What does "Probably not here" mean?

If something wasn't found at a store on two shops and you've never bought it there, it moves to the bottom of the list under "Probably not here" when you shop there, so you don't waste time looking. You can still tick it off if it's in. Items also say where they usually come from, such as "Usually from Aldi", when you mostly buy them at another store.

Why are some items under "Usual route"?

The app learns the order you pick things up in each store. Items with no aisle set follow that order, and items within an aisle do too, so the list matches how you walk round.

How do I scan a receipt?

Tap the … button, then Scan receipt. Scan it with the camera, or choose a photo of it. Each line with a price is matched to one of your items; check the matches (change any, or choose Skip), then tap Save. The prices are saved for that store, and anything still on your list comes off it, or is ticked off if you're shopping.

Can I set a budget?

Yes, in Settings › Budget, per shop, per month or both. The app adds up the prices you've noted on the items on your list (plus what you've spent so far this month, for a monthly budget) and says how far under or over you are. While shopping, the total turns orange as it nears the budget and red if it goes over, with a short message each time.

Stores

How do I add or choose a store?

Tap the store button at the top left. Choose a store from the list, or tap Manage stores to add, rename or delete them. Past shops, Trends and Settings are in the … button at the top right.

Can it choose the store for me?

Yes. While you're in a store, open Settings and tap the pin next to it to save where it is. After that, opening the app there chooses it for you, with Undo in case it chose wrongly. If you open the app in a supermarket you haven't saved, it offers to add it ("You're at Lidl, Holloway Road · Add"), once per shop. Turn both off with "Choose the store I'm in" in Settings.

Can I use Siri or widgets?

Yes. Say "Add to my Shopping List" and Siri asks what to add; you can name several things at once, such as "milk, bread and eggs". "What's on my Shopping List" reads it out, and "Start shopping with Shopping List" opens the app ready to tick things off. Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen show what's left to buy, and while you shop a Live Activity shows your progress on the Lock Screen.

How do I change the country for suggestions?

Suggestions while you type use the grocery names of your country, such as courgettes in the UK and zucchini in the US. Change it in Settings › Country, or run the setup guide again from Settings.

Your data

Where is my list kept?

Only on your iPhone, and in its backups. Nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

I've changed phone and my list is missing

Your list moves with your iPhone's backup. Restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one (iCloud or a computer) to bring it across.
